# Guide 1: Joseph's accounts, and what to do with each

**Who this is for:** Tristan, helping Joseph get set up.
**Rule:** every account is **Joseph's**, in his name with his payment details. Tristan helps him through it but never keeps Joseph's passwords.

Prices are approximate. Check each site before signing up.

## The accounts

| # | Account | Cost | Needed? | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Business email** (Gmail) | Free | Yes | One email for every account below, plus customer emails |
| 2 | **Shopify** | Basic plan ~$29–39/month, plus card fees per sale | Yes | The website, shop, checkout, blog, editor, analytics |
| 3 | **Shopify Payments** (inside Shopify) | Included | Yes | Lets him take card payments and get paid to his bank |
| 4 | **Print vendor:** Prodigi and/or Lumaprints (Shopify apps) | Free account; he pays the print cost per order | Yes | Prints, frames, and ships orders automatically |
| 5 | **Domain** (e.g. rowlandphotography.com) | ~$12–20/year | Yes | His web address |
| 6 | **GitHub** | Free | Yes | Stores the website's code |
| 7 | **Claude Pro** (Claude Code) or **ChatGPT Plus** (Codex) | ~$20/month | Optional | Only needed for design or code changes. Day-to-day edits don't need it |
| 8 | **Google Analytics** (via the free "Google & YouTube" Shopify app) | Free | Optional | Click-by-click analytics beyond Shopify's own |

**Monthly total:** about $30–40 for Shopify, plus about $20 if he keeps an AI subscription, plus about $15 a year for the domain.

## What to do, in order

### 1. Business email (5 min)
- Create a Gmail such as `rowlandphotography@gmail.com`.
- Use it for every account below.

### 2. GitHub account (5 min)
- Sign up free at **github.com**.
- Send Tristan the **username** (needed for the code transfer in Guide 3).

### 3. Shopify: receive the store (see Guide 3)
- Tristan transfers the store to Joseph's email.
- Joseph accepts, creates his Shopify login, and picks the **Basic** plan.

### 4. Shopify Payments (15 min, in the store)
- In **Settings → Payments**, activate **Shopify Payments**.
- He enters his legal name, address, date of birth, SSN (or EIN for a business), and his **bank account** for payouts.
- Also set up the store's own details:
  - **Settings → Store details:** business name, address, contact email.
  - **Settings → Policies:** use the generator for refund, privacy, terms, and shipping policies.
  - **Settings → Taxes and duties:** turn on automatic US sales tax.

### 5. Print vendor (30–60 min, plus waiting for samples)
1. **Apps → Shopify App Store**, search **Prodigi**, and install it. Optionally install **Lumaprints** too, to compare.
2. Create the vendor account through the app. Add a card for print costs.
3. **Order 1–2 sample prints** from each vendor, including a framed print with a mat. Compare quality before going live.
4. **Create products in the vendor app:** upload the full-resolution photo, pick paper, sizes, frames, and mats, set prices, and sync to Shopify.
   - **Name the options exactly `Size`, `Frame`, and `Mat`.** The site's live frame preview looks for those names.
5. In Shopify, add each product to a **collection** (e.g. "Landscapes"), so it appears on the Shop page.

### 6. Domain (10 min)
- **Simplest:** **Settings → Domains → Buy new domain** inside Shopify. Everything connects automatically.
- **Or** buy one at Cloudflare or Namecheap, then use **Settings → Domains → Connect existing domain**.

### 7. Optional: analytics and AI
- **Analytics:** Shopify's built-in **Analytics** page already shows visitors, top pages, and sales. For click detail, install **Google & YouTube** and connect a Google Analytics account.
- **AI:** if he wants an AI to make design changes, get **Claude Pro** or **ChatGPT Plus**, then follow **Guide 2** (`docs/AI_SETUP.md`).

### 8. Launch checklist
- [ ] At least a few products live with Size / Frame / Mat options
- [ ] Shopify Payments active
- [ ] A **test order** placed and refunded (Settings → Payments has a test mode)
- [ ] Policies filled in
- [ ] About page and blog posts in his own words
- [ ] Domain connected
- [ ] **Remove the password:** Online Store → Preferences → Password protection → off
